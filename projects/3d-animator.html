A lightweight tool for posing and animating 3D characters and objects made in Blender or other 3D tools. It runs in the browser or as a Windows app, with models and projects kept locally on your device.

Characters and scene elements can be arranged into shots with custom lighting, cameras and animation clips, without the memory overhead of a full 3D package such as Blender. The app is available in the browser, on Itch.io and the Microsoft Store. I still continue to maintain and add features to the app.

Features:
  • Load rigged .glb, .gltf and .fbx characters.
  • Pose bones and move, rotate or scale individual mesh parts with UI transform gizmos.
  • Change shape keys for facial expressions or other deformations during animation.
  • Play baked animations or create custom keyframe animations for bones, mesh parts, cameras, lights and root motion.
  • Import .bvh motion capture files, retarget them to a character rig and edit the resulting animation.
  • Add props and backgrounds, attach props to character bones, and arrange multiple cameras with keyframes and cuts.
  • Set up lighting and choose from shading styles including cell, toon, anime and realistic options.
  • Add cloth simulation, dangle-bone physics and ragdoll-style motion to characters.
  • Save and load poses, animation clips and scene layouts locally.
  • Export transparent PNGs, WebM videos, BVH animations and scene objects as .glb files.
